# Instructions for continued airworthiness — outline

**Status: outline.** This document defines the structure of the instructions for continued
airworthiness (ICA) for the installation; the intervals and the wear limits are not defined
yet, because they come with the platform maintenance programme.

**Requirement it satisfies**: 14 CFR 27.1529 and Appendix A to Part 27 (and the equivalent
CS-27 requirement) require the applicant to prepare instructions for continued airworthiness
for the rotorcraft, covering each installed appliance and its relation to the maintenance
programme. The installation kit is such an appliance; the payload is maintained under its own
documentation, and this ICA covers its attachment, interfaces and removal.

## A27.1 General

- **Applicability**: the EO/IR payload installation kit, all part numbers, all platforms on
  which the installation is approved.
- **System description**: interface frame, attachment fittings, payload interface plate,
  power and data harnesses, circuit protection, bonding, environmental provisions.
- **Abbreviations and references**: to the installation drawing, the structural analysis, the
  qualification plan and the payload interface control data.
- **Availability of data**: where the approved documents live and who maintains them.

## A27.2 Limitations

- Airworthiness limitations, if the change classification produces any: none identified at
  this stage; the section stays open until the classification is closed.
- Life limits of the fittings and the fasteners, if any: to be defined with the structural
  analysis.

## A27.3 Servicing

- Payload installation and removal procedure, including the two independent locking devices
  per fastener and the torque values.
- Connector mating and unmating, with the handling rules and the mating cycle limit.
- Bonding strap inspection and refitting.
- Cleaning and protection of the exposed surfaces, with the fluids the installation resists.

## A27.4 Scheduled inspections

| Item | What is inspected | Interval |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Attachment fittings | Cracks, corrosion, edge damage, deformed holes | to be defined with the platform |
| Fasteners and locking devices | Torque, locking device integrity, corrosion | to be defined with the platform |
| Interface frame | Cracks, corrosion, paint condition, drain paths | to be defined with the platform |
| Harnesses and connectors | Chafing, clamping, bend radius, connector seating, screen continuity | to be defined with the platform |
| Bonding path | Resistance of the bonding straps (2.5 mΩ limit) | to be defined with the platform |
| Payload interface plate | Hole elongation, plate flatness, corrosion | to be defined with the platform |

## A27.5 Wear items and replacement

- Replaceable items: fasteners and locking devices, bonding straps, clamps, connector seals.
- Payload replacement: the payload is a line-replaceable unit; the removal corridor and the
  connector access are declared in the layout drawing.
- Consumables and their acceptance data.

## A27.6 Structural repair

- Allowed repairs and the conditions under which a repair becomes a new design change under
  Part 21.
- Fitting replacement procedure and the check that the replacement keeps the fitting factor
  and the edge distances required by the design rules.

## A27.7 Troubleshooting

- Loss of payload power, intermittent data, bonding resistance above the limit, vibration
  symptoms, visual damage. Symptom, probable cause, check, corrective action.

## A27.8 Parts list

- Illustrated parts list with part numbers, revisions and the supplier reference where one
  exists (none declared at this stage: the design is notional).
