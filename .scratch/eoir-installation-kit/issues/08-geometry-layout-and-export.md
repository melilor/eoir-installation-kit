## Answer

Closed on 2026-09-18.

**The layout is one file.** `model/layout.yaml` at revision A is the single source of truth for
every dimension: a two-view 2D envelope model in millimetres (side view and bottom view) with
rectangles, circles and orthogonal harness paths, plus the declared design rules and the
interface references the layout has to agree with. The 2D simplification is declared in the
file and in the README, not hidden.

**Twelve checks, 89 assertions.** `tools/layout.py` runs `REMOVAL-CORRIDOR`,
`CONNECTOR-ACCESS`, `FITTING-EDGE-DISTANCE`, `HOLE-PITCH`, `FASTENER-LOCKING`,
`INSPECTION-ACCESS`, `HARNESS-ORTHOGONAL`, `BEND-RADIUS`, `CLAMP-ON-PATH`, `CLAMP-SPACING`,
`HARNESS-CLEARANCE` and `ICD-CONSISTENCY`. Each finding carries the check identifier, the
subject, the measured value, the limit and the margin, so a failure names the violated
constraint instead of reporting a feeling. The tool exits non-zero on the first failing run
and prints the failing subjects.

The checks earned their place immediately: the first run failed on four clamp spacings
(300 mm, 210 mm, 240 mm and 270 mm against a 200 mm rule) and on one non-orthogonal harness
segment in the declared data. Both were layout errors, both were fixed in the layout, not in
the checks.

**Evidence.** `evidence/layout/` holds four generated files: `checks.json` (machine
readable), `report.md` (the table with values and margins), `layout.svg` (the two views) and
`layout.dxf` (a DXF R12 ASCII export with both views, one layer per element class). CI runs
`python -m tools.layout --check`, so the committed evidence cannot differ from the model.

**Verification cases closed at the design-definition level**

| Case | Status | What the evidence shows | What it does not |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| VER008 harness routing and pinout | `PASS` | routing, clamping, bend radius, connector access and the interface references agree with the declared rules and the ICD | the installed harness on an article |
| VER013 maintainability | `LIMITATION` | removal corridor free and inspection access preserved | the 30-minute, two-maintainer target without a first article |
| VER019 fastener locking | `PASS` | every fastener declares two independent locking devices | procurement and installation |
| VER021 inspection provisions | `PASS` | the inspection zones stay clear of the payload footprint | access with the payload installed on the platform |
| VER023 harness installation rules | `PASS` | clearance to hot zones and moving parts, clamp spacing, bend radius | clamp installation on the structure |

Three `ASM-OPEN` warnings appear, by design: the evidence rests on the harness rules (`A-012`,
used by `SYS030`/`SYS031`) and on the payload video link (`A-007`, used by `SYS012`), both still
open.

Model state: 38 requirements, 28 verification cases, 12 components, 13 assumptions, 10 rules,
89 layout checks, 55 tests. Validator: 0 errors, 3 warnings.

**One mismatch found and not hidden**: the platform ICD declares attachment hard points 900 mm
apart longitudinally and 300 mm laterally, while the first draft of the layout placed them
340 mm apart. The layout was rebuilt on the declared hard points, and the `ICD-CONSISTENCY`
check now fails if either document drifts.
